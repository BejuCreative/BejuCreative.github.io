What is a done-for-you content system?

Beju Creative's Content Engine is a done-for-you content system: a content operation the studio builds and runs for you. You record once and talk to the camera, and the studio handles the ideas, the edits and the captions, so finished Reels come out all week.

What is Beju Creative?

Beju Creative is a video editing studio based in Lithuania that works with creators, coaches, podcasters and SaaS teams worldwide. The studio edits short-form videos for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, edits long videos and podcast episodes, produces launch films, and runs content systems for clients. Work starts with a free sample, and revisions are unlimited.

How much does the Content Engine cost?

Beju Creative quotes the Content Engine per channel. The price depends on how many videos you need each month, how long they are and how much motion design goes into them. We review every application by hand and reply within one hour.

Is this the same as short-form editing?

Short-form editing cuts Shorts and Reels from videos you already have. The Content Engine is a system the studio builds and runs around your recordings. If you only need edits, see short-form video editing.

Is my footage kept private?

Yes. Footage stays private. Upload folders are shared only with you, and raw files are deleted once the edits are approved.
