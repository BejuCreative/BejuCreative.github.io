Video editing service FAQ.

Answers to what people ask before they start with a video editing service: the free sample, working with us, pricing and your footage.

Apply for a free sample Book a call

The free sample.

What is the free sample?

We edit a Short from a video you already have and send it to you. No payment, no card. We review every application by hand, so we cannot promise that everyone gets a sample. Apply for a free sample.

How do I apply for a free sample?

Fill in the application on our free sample page. Tell us about your content and the style you like. It takes two minutes.

How fast do you reply?

We reply to every application within one hour.

Working with us.

Who is Beju Creative for?

Creators, coaches, podcasters, course creators, SaaS and app teams, and e-commerce brands that publish often and want their editing handled by one team.

What do you edit?

Short-form videos for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, cut from your long videos in your own style. We also edit long videos and podcast episodes, make launch films for SaaS and app launches, and build and run The Content Engine. For e-commerce brands we make ad creatives from real reviews and footage.

Do you edit long videos too?

Yes. We edit full-length videos and podcast episodes as well as Shorts and Reels.

How fast do you deliver?

We deliver everything within 24 hours once we have your footage and brief, and usually faster. That includes weekends.

What happens after the free sample?

If you like it, you send us your recordings, and finished edits come back in your style. We quote per channel, so you see the work before you see a quote.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. Email hello@bejucreative.digital or book a 15-minute call. You can also see our work first. We have worked with Sam Zia's team and BASED.

Pricing and payment.

How does pricing work?

We quote per channel. The price depends on how many videos you need each month, how long they are and how much motion design goes into them. It starts with a free sample, so you see the work before you see a quote.

What if I want changes?

Ask for them. Revisions are unlimited, and we keep editing until you're happy with the result. Revisions cover changes to the edit of the footage you sent. New footage or a new brief counts as a new order. The details are in our Terms.

How do I pay?

Payments run through Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. Section 6 of our Terms explains how payment works.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes. You can cancel any order until we start work on it, and if you are buying for yourself and not for a business, you also have a 14-day right to cancel. Section 8 of our Terms sets out the details.

Your footage.

Is my footage safe with you?

Yes. Your footage stays private, upload folders are shared only with you, and raw files are deleted once your edits are approved. Our Privacy Policy explains what we keep and for how long.

How do I send you my recordings?

If your files are not online, we email you an upload folder that is shared only with your email address. If they are already online, send us the link.

Who owns the finished videos?

You do. You keep ownership of your original footage, and once an order is paid in full you own the edited videos we deliver. Section 7 of our Terms has the full wording.

Will my videos appear in your portfolio?

We may show finished work, or anonymised clips of it, in our portfolio unless you tell us otherwise. To opt out, email hello@bejucreative.digital and say "private". You can do this at any time.
