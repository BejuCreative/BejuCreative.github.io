How working with us works, step by step. You're working directly with Benas and Simas, the two editors cutting your videos. Three minutes to read.

How it works

Six steps, start to finish

  1. You get a private Google Drive folder. The link is in your welcome email from bejusipe@gmail.com.
  2. You send your footage into its Footage uploads folder.
  3. You tell us your style (optional). Skip it and we'll edit in our proven style.
  4. We edit and put your finished videos in your Drive folder.
  5. You check them and send any changes. One round of changes is included.
  6. You post. The clips are yours to post, reuse or run as ads.

Step 1

Sending your footage

How to send it
  • Open the Footage uploads folder from your welcome email and drag your files in.
  • No folder link yet, or prefer another way? Send a WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive link by email or WhatsApp.
  • Big files take a while. Keep the tab open until the upload says it's complete.
What to send
  • The original video file, straight from your camera, Riverside, Zoom or editing app. Files sent through WhatsApp, Instagram or a screen recording lose quality.
  • The whole recording. Finding the best moments is our job.
  • Notes: favourite moments with times (like "12:30"), names to spell right, anything we should never show or say.

Want your first edits to land perfectly? Our one-minute style form asks for all of this in one place.

Steps 4–5

Getting your videos

Short-form packages arrive within 24 hours, long-form packages within 48 hours, after payment, usable footage and the brief are confirmed. If a file is broken or has no sound, we'll ask you to send it again before the clock starts.

On a computer
  • Open your Drive folder. Each order has its own folder, like 5 short · 2026-10-02.
  • Click a video to play it in your browser. Just uploaded? Big videos can take a few minutes before they play.
  • To download: right-click a video, then Download. Select several with Ctrl (Windows) or Command (Mac).
On your phone
  • Install the free Google Drive app, then open your folder link.
  • iPhone: tap ⋮ next to the video, then Send a copy, then Save video.
  • Android: tap ⋮ next to the video, then Download.

Links never expire. Your videos stay in your folder. When you post, upload the video file to each app; don't share the Drive link.

Step 5

Asking for changes

Each order includes one round of revisions. Send all your changes for the order together and we'll fix them. A substantially different edit from the original brief may be treated as a new order.

Option A · comment in Google Drive
  1. On a computer, open the video in your Drive folder, signed in to your Google account.
  2. Pause at the exact moment, click Comment, type your note, click Comment.
  3. Repeat for each change, then message us: "Comments are in."

Timed comments work in a desktop browser only. On your phone, use Option B.

Option B · one message

Email, WhatsApp or Instagram. Give the clip number and the time:

Example

Clip 2 — 0:04 cut the pause
Clip 2 — 0:18 her name is "Sara"
Clip 4 — use the other hook

We put the updated videos in your client folder.

Short-form packages

The 2× views guarantee

Double your agreed baseline average views, or receive a full refund of the qualifying short-form package payment. Long-form editing and launch films are not covered.

  • Your baseline is set before you pay. We note the public view counts of your last 10 clips on one account that are at least 30 days old, and you confirm their average. With fewer than three such clips, the guarantee doesn't apply, and we tell you before you pay.
  • Post on time. Publish each delivered clip within 14 days on the agreed account, and keep it public and unchanged, with no paid promotion, for its first 30 days.
  • We measure the package average. Each clip's views in its first 30 days after posting, divided by the number of clips. Individual clips don't each have to double.
  • Claim within seven days after the last clip's 30-day window ends: email your order, clip links and posting dates to hello@bejucreative.digital.

Tip: screenshot your views as you go. Read the full guarantee rules; the terms agreed for your order apply.
