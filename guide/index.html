Six steps, start to finish

  1. You tell us what to edit at checkout. Your channel or video links are all we need. We email you within 12 hours, 7 days a week, to confirm your order.
  2. Files not online? Upload them to the upload folder we email you. It's shared only with your email address.
  3. You tell us your style (optional). Skip it and we'll edit in our proven style.
  4. We edit and put your finished videos in a Google Drive folder for your order.
  5. You check them and send any changes within 48 hours. Revisions are unlimited. We keep editing until you're happy.
  6. You post. The clips are yours to post, reuse or run as ads.

Sending your footage

How to send it

  • Already online? The links you gave us at checkout are enough. There's nothing to upload.
  • Files not online? Open the upload folder we email you and drag your files in. It's shared only with your email address, so open it signed in to that Google account.
  • No Google account, no folder email yet, or prefer another way? Send a WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive link by email or WhatsApp.
  • Big files take a while. Keep the tab open until the upload says it's complete.

What to send

  • The original video file, straight from your camera, Riverside, Zoom or editing app. Files sent through WhatsApp, Instagram or a screen recording lose quality.
  • The whole recording. Finding the best moments is our job.
  • Notes: favourite moments with times (like "12:30"), names to spell right, anything we should never show or say.

Want your first edits to land perfectly? Our one-minute style form asks for all of this in one place.

Packs expire 14 days after you pay, so send your footage before then (section 2.4 of our terms).

Getting your videos

We deliver everything within 24 hours, and usually faster, after payment, usable footage and the brief are confirmed. Weekends and holidays included. If a file is broken or has no sound, we'll ask you to send it again before the clock starts.

On a computer

  • Open your Drive folder. Each order has its own folder, like 5 Shorts · Order 1 · 2026-10-02.
  • Click a video to play it in your browser. Just uploaded? Big videos can take a few minutes before they play.
  • To download: right-click a video, then Download. Select several with Ctrl (Windows) or Command (Mac).

On your phone

  • Install the free Google Drive app, then open your folder link.
  • iPhone: tap ⋮ next to the video, then Send a copy, then Save video.
  • Android: tap ⋮ next to the video, then Download.

Links never expire. Your finished videos stay in your folder. When you post, upload the video file to each app; don't share the Drive link. We delete your raw footage once you've approved your edits, so keep your own copy of the originals.

Asking for changes

Revisions are unlimited. We keep editing until you're happy. Send all your changes for the order together, within 48 hours of delivery, and we'll fix them. A substantially different edit from the original brief may be treated as a new order.

Option A · comment in Google Drive

  1. On a computer, open the video in your Drive folder, signed in to your Google account.
  2. Pause at the exact moment, click Comment, type your note, click Comment.
  3. Repeat for each change, then message us: "Comments are in."

Timed comments work in a desktop browser only. On your phone, use Option B.

Option B · one message

Email, WhatsApp or Instagram. Give the clip number and the time:

Example
Clip 2, 0:04: cut the pause
Clip 2, 0:18: her name is "Sara"
Clip 4: use the other hook

We put the updated videos in your client folder.
