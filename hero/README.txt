not a client's brand.

    client's podcast with "trakyo pod" on screen. Don't repeat that.

    seamless-ish. Punchy hook, visible captions, your style.

    clips open on a white flash, which looks broken in the phone.

HOW TO CHECK IT'S REALLY VERTICAL
---------------------------------
Right-click the file > Get Info. Dimensions should say 1080 x 1920.
If it says 1920 x 1080, it's landscape and will look wrong.

If it SAYS 1080x1920 but still has black bars top and bottom when
you play it, that's the letterbox problem — re-export it filling
the full vertical frame.
