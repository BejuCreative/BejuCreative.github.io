How it works

Three steps, no call needed

If anything's missing, we email you.

  1. Pick a pack and pay

    Pay on Stripe's secure checkout by card, or with Apple Pay or Google Pay when your device supports them. We never see your full card details. We email you within 12 hours, 7 days a week, to confirm your order.

  2. Tell us what to edit

    At checkout, paste your channel and we'll pick the strongest moments, or paste the exact videos you want edited. Files not online yet? Type upload, and after you pay we email you an upload folder, shared only with your email address.

  3. Get your Shorts

    They land in a Google Drive folder for your order within 24 hours, long videos within 48, once we have working footage and your brief. Weekends and holidays included. One round of changes is included if you ask within 48 hours of delivery, and the files are yours to post anywhere.

Questions

Before you order

Anything else? Email hello@bejucreative.digital. We reply within 24 hours.

What footage do you need from me?

Whatever you already have: a podcast recording, a coaching call, or a talk to camera. We cut it into short clips. You don't need to film anything new, write scripts or prepare shot lists.

My videos aren’t online. How do I send them?

Type upload in the checkout box. After you pay, we email you an upload folder, shared only with your email address, and your delivery time starts once your files are in.

Is checkout safe?

Yes. Payment runs on Stripe, and we never see your full card details. Pay by card, or with Apple Pay or Google Pay when your device supports them. Prices are in US dollars.

Can I get a refund?

You can cancel for a full refund until we start work. If you buy as a consumer, you also have a 14-day right to cancel (section 8 of our Terms): if you cancel after we have started, you pay only for the videos already delivered, and the right ends once your videos are fully delivered. Packs expire 14 days after you pay: if by then we still have no usable footage from you, or you've stopped replying, we close the order and the videos not yet made lapse (section 2.4). Your first Shorts pack, with a baseline we agreed before you ordered, is also covered by our 2× views guarantee (section 5). This doesn't limit your rights under the law.

Do you guarantee results?

Yes, on your first Shorts pack, where we agree your baseline before you order. If the average views of your pack's Shorts don't reach twice that baseline, we refund the pack price. The guarantee covers your first order only. The posting rules and how to claim are in section 5 of our Terms. Want it? Send us your channel before you order and we'll confirm your baseline within 24 hours.
