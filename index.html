Our mission

Good ideas
shouldn't lose
to better editing

The best coach in your city is probably losing to someone half as good with a better editor. That's the part nobody says out loud. The algorithm doesn't rank expertise — it ranks retention. And retention is a craft.

We started BEJÚ because that gap felt fixable. Most people who need this work can't justify $2,000 a month for it, so they either post something unedited or they don't post at all. Both lose. We're two editors, no account managers, no agency overhead — which is the entire reason we can charge what we charge and still hand back work we'd put our name on.

So we made the offer simple: send us one video and we'll edit it free. No call, no contract, no pitch deck. We'd rather prove it than describe it.

Questions

Before you ask

How much does short-form video editing cost?

Short-form edits start at $27 for a single video, $60 for three, and $75 for five — $15 per edit at the five-video tier. Long-form is priced per finished video, from $75. Prices vary with the length of the source footage; these are averages for 15–40 second reels.

Do you offer a free sample edit?

Yes. Send us one video and we'll edit a clip from it free, with no call and no contract. You see the quality before you spend anything.

How fast is the turnaround?

24 hours on average, from the moment you send footage to a finished clip ready to post. Revisions come back the same day.

What happens if the edits don't perform?

If our edits don't at least double your average views within 30 days of posting, we refund you in full — no credits, no vouchers, no questions.

What footage do you need from me?

Whatever you already have — a podcast recording, a coaching call, or a talk to camera. We cut it into short clips. You don't need to film anything new, write scripts or prepare shot lists.

Which platforms do you format for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn. Every clip is exported and sized for each platform, with captions burned in.
