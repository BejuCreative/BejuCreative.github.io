Questions.

How does pricing work?

We quote per channel. The price depends on how many videos you need each month, how long they are and how much motion design goes into them. It starts with a free sample, so you see the work before you see a quote. Our guide explains how video editing is priced.

How fast do you reply?

We reply to every application within one hour.

How fast do you deliver?

We deliver everything within 24 hours once we have your footage and brief, and usually faster. That includes weekends.

What if I want changes?

Ask for them. Revisions are unlimited, and we keep editing until you're happy with the result.

Do you edit long videos too?

Yes. We edit full-length videos and podcast episodes as well as Shorts and Reels.

Is my footage safe with you?

Yes. Your footage stays private, upload folders are shared only with you, and raw files are deleted once your edits are approved. Payments run through Stripe.

Who is this for?

Creators, coaches, podcasters, course creators, SaaS and app teams, and e-commerce brands that publish often and want their editing handled by one team.

What is Beju Creative?

Beju Creative is a video editing studio based in Lithuania that works with creators, coaches, podcasters and SaaS teams worldwide. The studio edits short-form videos for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, edits long videos and podcast episodes, produces launch films, and runs content systems for clients.
