What is podcast clip editing?

Beju Creative's podcast clip editing service cuts the strongest moments of each episode into Shorts and Reels. We choose moments that make sense on their own, keep the question when the answer needs it, add captions and sound, and finish every clip on a complete thought. We edit full episodes as well.

How much does podcast clip editing cost?

Beju Creative quotes its podcast clip editing service per channel. The price depends on how many videos you need each month, how long they are and how much motion design goes into them. Podcast questions.

Do we choose the moments, or do you?

Either. Send timestamps for the moments you want, or leave it to us. We watch the episode, pick the strongest moments and send you the clips. Tell us which topics matter most and we start there.

Will a clip make sense without the full episode?

That is what we edit for. When an answer needs its question, we keep the question or a short setup. We cut detours and repeats, and each clip ends on a finished thought.

Do you edit full episodes as well?

Yes. We edit full-length videos and podcast episodes as well as Shorts and Reels. Tell us in your application whether you need clips, full episodes or both.

Can you work from one recording?

Yes. Send the recording you have, whether it is one file or several. More camera angles give more room to work with, but they are not required.

Is my footage safe with you?

Yes. Your footage stays private, upload folders are shared only with you, and raw files are deleted once your edits are approved. Payments run through Stripe.
