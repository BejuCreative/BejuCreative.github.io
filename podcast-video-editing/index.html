A podcast clip should make sense without the episode

An interesting answer can lose its meaning when the question disappears. Our approach to podcast clips is to keep the context that matters, remove detours, and build toward one clear takeaway. The aim is a concise edit that still sounds like your guest.

This is useful for interview shows, business conversations and educational podcasts that want to share selected moments on Instagram Reels, TikTok or YouTube Shorts.

What matters in an interview edit

  • Context: preserve the question or a short setup when the answer needs it.
  • Speaker framing: keep the relevant speaker visible when adapting a landscape recording for a vertical screen.
  • Natural pacing: cut repetition without making the conversation feel disconnected.
  • Accurate captions: supply guest names, company names and specialist terms so they can be checked.
  • A complete ending: finish the thought instead of cutting off simply to hit a duration.

If a statement depends on a qualification later in the conversation, include that context in the brief. A stronger hook should not turn a nuanced point into a misleading claim.

How to brief your podcast clips

  1. Share the episode video or original files and any separate speaker recordings or audio.
  2. Tell us who the show is for and which episode ideas you want to highlight.
  3. Send selected timestamps if you have them, plus examples of your preferred editing style.
  4. Confirm clip quantity, target platforms and any guest approval requirements.

A YouTube link helps us review the episode, but tell us whether you can provide original footage for the final edit. Source quality affects the finished clips. When reviewing, give timestamped notes and consolidate host and guest feedback where possible.

Podcast editing questions

Can you work from a single-camera recording?

Yes. Share the file so we can assess framing and audio. Separate camera angles give more flexibility, but they are not a requirement for discussing an edit.

Do you also offer long-form editing?

Long-form editing is listed in our current packages. Send your episode length and requirements so the scope can be confirmed.

How many clips should I request?

Choose a quantity based on the number of useful standalone moments and your publishing needs. Not every segment of an episode needs to become a short.
