Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. The short version

  • We collect only what we need to edit your videos and talk to you.
  • We don't sell your data. Our website has no analytics, no ad trackers and no cookies of its own. The bot check on the sample form is explained in section 11.
  • Stripe handles payments. We never see your full card details.
  • Your files live in Google Drive folders we control and share with you.
  • You can ask us to see or delete your data at any time.

2. Who we are

BEJÚ Creative ("BEJÚ," "we," "us") is a two-person video editing studio based in Lithuania. We decide how the information on this page is used, so we're responsible for it.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It sits alongside our Terms of service. Questions? Email hello@bejucreative.digital.

3. What we collect

  • Your name and email. From the /brief form or when you email or message us. Stripe also gives us your name and email when you pay.
  • Your order details. What you ordered, and the links and notes you give us in the /brief form and in emails: your channel or the exact videos to edit, a link to your footage, and anything we should know about style, must-use moments or deadlines.
  • Form answers. What you type into our free sample form (/brief): your channel link, your email, how often you post and what you sell, and, if you fill in the optional style step, your name, the video and moment to work from, your preferred look, captions, energy and graphics, brand colours, font and logo link, reference clips, anything to avoid, music and notes. Our optional style form on the welcome page collects similar details (handles, reference clips, style notes and anything we should never show).
  • Public channel details. For the channel you give us, we look at public information, such as the subscriber count, how often you post and your recent videos, to check the request and to prepare your sample.
  • Request records. The date of your request, whether you confirmed your email, and the status of your request.
  • Your footage. The files you upload or link to, and what's in them, such as faces, voices and names.
  • Messages and feedback. Emails, WhatsApp messages and Instagram DMs you send us, and comments you leave on your edits in Google Drive.
  • Guarantee records. If your order is covered by our performance guarantee: the agreed account, the public view counts of your clips, and the analytics you send with a claim.
  • Chat questions. Our website assistant, Ju, runs entirely in your browser. It doesn't send, save or share anything you type, and the conversation clears when you leave the page. If you then email us or use a form, we receive only what you send.

Tips and updates. Our sample forms have an optional box, "Yes, email me BEJÚ tips and updates". It is never ticked for you. If you tick it, we add your email to our updates list and store that choice with your form answers in our Google Sheet. Every update email lets you unsubscribe, or reply "unsubscribe" and we take you off the list.

We sometimes email creators whose business email is public (on YouTube, Instagram or their website) to offer our editing. Reply "stop" and we won't email you again.

4. Why we use it

  • To make your free sample and quote. Checking your request, confirming your email, making your sample and sending your quote.
  • To do the work you ordered. Editing, delivering your videos, revisions and the guarantee.
  • To talk to you. Replying to questions and sending updates about your order.
  • To keep records the law requires. Payments and accounting.
  • To show our work. Featuring finished edits in our portfolio, unless you opt out (see section 9).

Our legal reason for each use

  • Making your free sample and sending your quote: you asked for them, so we need your details to take those steps before any order.
  • Protecting the sample form from bots and abuse (the bot check, the hidden trap field and the one-request-per-email limit): our legitimate interest in keeping the form working.
  • Doing the work you ordered (editing, delivery, your round of changes and the guarantee): it is needed to carry out our contract with you.
  • Payment and accounting records: the law requires us to keep them.
  • Answering your messages and enquiries, showing our finished work and running our business: our legitimate interest. You can object at any time, and we will stop unless we have a strong reason to continue.
  • Emailing creators whose business email is public (see section 3): our legitimate interest in offering our editing.
  • Consent: if we ever ask for your consent, you can take it back at any time by emailing us. Taking it back doesn't affect what we did before.

To ask for a sample, you need to give us your channel link, your email, how often you post and what you sell. To place an order, we also need the links to your videos (or your files). Without them we can't do the work. We don't make decisions about you by computer alone.

5. Payments

When you accept a quote, we send you a payment link, and Stripe processes the payment. Stripe handles your card, Apple Pay or Google Pay details. We never see or store your full card details.

Stripe shares with us what we need to run your order, such as your name, email, what you bought and the amount. Stripe handles your payment data under its own privacy policy.

6. Where your files and data live

Your footage and finished edits live in Google Drive folders in our account. If your files aren't online, we email you an upload folder, shared only with your email address, never by link. Your finished edits go into your Google Drive client folder for the order, and we email you when they're ready.

We share delivery folders by link, so anyone who has the link can open them. Please keep the link to yourself.

To run the business we use a few trusted services, each under its own privacy terms:

  • Stripe for payments made through our payment links.
  • Google for email (Gmail), files (Google Drive), our welcome-page form (Google Forms), and Google Sheets and Apps Script, which store your sample form answers and send the confirmation email from our Google account.
  • Cloudflare for the bot check (Turnstile) on the sample form, and for a code library (cdnjs) described in section 11.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram, if you choose to message us there.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website (see section 11).
  • Tally and cal.com, if you use our call application form or book a call. They load only when you click.
  • Instantly and Gmail, for the outreach emails described in section 3.

Several of these services are based in, or store data in, the United States. We rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified, and on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission where it is not.

7. Our website forms

When you send the free sample form (/brief), your answers go to a Google Apps Script web app in our Google account. It checks the bot check with Cloudflare, saves your answers as a row in a Google Sheet we own, and sends you a confirmation email from our Google account with a link to confirm that it is you. Google sees your IP address when your answers reach it. The form also has a hidden field that real visitors never see; if it is filled in, we treat the request as a bot and drop it. We allow one request per email address every 7 days, and we check the Sheet for earlier requests to do that. We act on a request once you have confirmed your email.

The optional style form on our welcome page is a Google Form. Its answers are stored in our Google account too. Only our team can see the Sheet and the form answers. Google handles them under its own privacy policy.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your raw footage. The files you upload and any copies we download from your links. We delete them once you've approved your edits: when you tell us they're OK, or when the 48-hour window for changes ends with no request.
  • Your finished edits. They stay in your Google Drive client folder, so you can download them any time. Ask us and we'll delete the folder.
  • Payment records. As long as Lithuanian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Emails, messages and form answers. While they're useful for your project or enquiry. If you never order, we delete your enquiry and form answers after 12 months. Ask us and we'll delete them sooner.

9. Showing your work

Under section 7 of our terms, we may show finished work, or anonymized clips of it, in our portfolio and marketing, unless you tell us otherwise.

To opt out, email hello@bejucreative.digital and say "private" or "no portfolio". You can do this at any time, before or after delivery. We'll stop using your work in anything new and take it off our website and our social accounts.

10. We don't sell your data

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with the services listed in section 6 that help us run the business, or when the law requires it.

11. Cookies and analytics

Our own pages don't set any cookies. They have no analytics, no ad pixels and no tracking scripts. The one outside check is the bot check described below.

  • One small setting. If you pause the video previews, your browser remembers that until you close the tab (using session storage). It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Bot check. The last step of the free sample form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. It loads only there. Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details, and may set or read a cookie or similar browser storage to run the check, under its own privacy policy.
  • An animation library. To scroll smoothly, our pages load a small script (GSAP) from cdnjs, a free code library service run by Cloudflare. Like any web request, Cloudflare sees your IP address when it sends the file. If your device asks for reduced motion, the script doesn't load.
  • Hosting. GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. We can't see those logs.
  • Stripe and Google pages. Payment links open on Stripe's site, and our style form opens on Google's. Those pages may use their own cookies under their own policies.

  • stop or limit how we use it, including portfolio use;
  • object to our use of your data for our legitimate interests, and, where we rely on your consent, take that consent back at any time;
  • give you your data in a form you can move to another service, where the law gives you that right.

Email hello@bejucreative.digital, message us on WhatsApp at +370 695 75791, or DM @bejucreative on Instagram. We reply within 24 hours and complete your request within one month. We may need to keep some records, like payment records, if the law says so.
