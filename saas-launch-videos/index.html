One clear idea, shown fast

A launch video has one job: make someone understand what your product does, quickly. We plan the edit around a single idea — what the product is, the moment it solves the problem, and what the viewer should do next — and cut everything that doesn’t serve it.

Pacing, motion, sound and on-screen text all support that idea, so the same film works on your launch post, your landing page and in a feed.

What to send us

  • Your product: a link, a demo account or a build we can explore.
  • Screens to feature: screen recordings or design files of the flows you want to show.
  • Brand assets: logo, fonts, colours and any existing guidelines.
  • Where it will run: launch post, landing page, Product Hunt or socials, so the formats are planned from the start.
  • References and timing: one or two launch videos you like, and your launch date.

Formats for every placement

Tell us where the video will live. A 16:9 cut suits landing pages, YouTube and X; a square or vertical cut fits social feeds. We agree the formats before we start, so every version is designed for its screen rather than cropped afterwards.

Pricing: a custom quote per launch

Launch videos are quoted per project, because length, motion work and deadlines vary from launch to launch. Send us your brief and we’ll reply with a quote within 24 hours.

Launch video questions

Do you make launch videos for apps as well as SaaS?

Yes. We make launch videos for SaaS products, apps and other product launches.

How long should a launch video be?

Long enough to land one idea, short enough to hold attention in a feed. We’ll suggest a length once we’ve seen your product.

Can you also edit short clips for our socials?

Yes. For founder videos, interviews and talking-head content, see our short-form video editing or podcast clip editing.
