03The path

From a Short to a booking, step by step

Your answer to "how would it work". This is one funnel. Once it's set up, it runs by itself, and each month we build one more for another offer. YouTube doesn't send private messages from comments, so the path runs through one link.

1

Someone watches your Short

A Short about getting off Auto, a sunrise at Chesterton Windmill, or reading the histogram. At the end you tell them where to go next.

2

They tap one link

The link sits in the description and in a pinned comment. It opens a short page on your own site for that topic, with a free gift. For the first funnel, that's your free Academy 101 course.

3

They give their email for your free course

They type their name and email address, tick the consent box and confirm. They land in your Mailchimp list, tagged with the Short they came from.

4

Five emails in your voice

A lesson from the course, a real student story, then the course, workshop or 1-2-1 lessons that fit what they watched, with your next dates. We write them, you approve them once.

5

They book

A beginners course, a workshop, 1-2-1 lessons, the Academy year, or a free chat in your calendar. Each booking is traced back to the Short that started it.

Your Shorts keep working after the views.

Today a view is the end of the story. With one link and one gift, every Short you've already scheduled can bring a name to your list and a booking to your calendar.

Why your free course works as the gift

People learn from you before they pay you, so trust starts early. And everyone who asks for the course has already shown they want to learn photography.

Each card says what we do, what your part is, and gives one example.

01

New Shorts for each funnel

What we do

We make 15 new Shorts a month from your long videos, talks and workshop footage, each one pointing to that month's funnel. Before we build each one, you get a written proposal: timecodes, opening and closing words, the images and the length. Each Short ends by telling viewers where to go next.

Your part

OK each proposal, or change it.

A Short on getting off Auto, cut from your beginners teaching, ends with "Get my free course, link below".

02

What we do

Every Short, new or already scheduled, gets its own tagged link in the description and a pinned comment. The link opens the topic page on your site.

Your part

Nothing. We add the links once you've given us access.

Example

Your Short on reading the histogram links to the exposure page, which offers your free Academy 101 course.

03

What we do

Each month, one offer you pick gets its own funnel: a short page in your Squarespace, in your look, with a free gift and the sign-up form that feeds your Mailchimp list, plus its emails and Shorts. Every funnel keeps running after its month.

Your part

Example

Month 1, the Academy: your free course as the gift. Month 2, your private lessons. Month 3, your Pick n Mix plan. You choose the order.

12Good to know

Questions

Do I pay for any software?

No. We build inside your Squarespace site, your Mailchimp list and your booking calendar. If a step needs a tool you don't have, our team pays for it and runs it.

Will this duplicate my Academy, my emails or my list?

No. New contacts join your existing Mailchimp list, tagged with their Short. Your blog updates keep going as they do today. The welcome series only runs for new contacts from Shorts, and it points to your own Academy, courses and workshops.

How do you handle consent?

Every sign-up ticks a consent box and confirms their address, the same way your list already works. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We only use what people share on purpose: a name and an email address.

How will I know what's working?

Every Short has its own tagged link, and every sign-up carries the name of its Short. When someone books, we match it back. The monthly report shows clicks, sign-ups, enquiries and bookings, per Short and per funnel.

I already have Shorts scheduled. What happens to them?

They keep going out as planned. We add a link to each one, so they bring sign-ups from the first day. Our new Shorts follow on your calendar.

Which offer gets the first funnel?

You choose. We'd suggest the Academy, since your Shorts already end on your free course. Then the offers you most want to fill, one a month.

Do I still get a proposal before each Short?

Yes. Timecodes, opening and closing words, the images and the length, before we build it.

Who owns my site, my list and my videos?

You do. Your site, your Mailchimp list, your Academy, your social accounts and every video stay yours.

Can I move to the higher tier plan later?

Yes. Email us, and it starts from your next billing date. Everything we built keeps running. The 30-day promise starts the day you pay for the higher tier plan: if no booking is traced to the system in those 30 days, you choose one more month free or that month back.

What if I don't like a Short or an email?

Tell us, and we change it until it's right. Revisions are unlimited.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month-to-month. Email us before your next billing date. You keep everything delivered. No refunds on delivered work, except the 30-day promise in the higher tier plan.
