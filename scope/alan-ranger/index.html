02One example

How a new person finds you: one example

Here is one funnel end to end, for your winter woodland workshops, built on a Short you already have.

1

Someone watches your Short "How much negative space?" (Lesson 30, the three Hay Wood photographs).

2

The end card shows a short web address for this Short: alanranger.com/woodland. On Instagram and Facebook the caption says: comment WOODLAND and we'll send you the link.

3

On Instagram or Facebook, they comment the word and get a private message from you within a minute, with the link.

4

The link opens a page on your own site: "Photographing woodland in winter". It explains the idea from the Short in a few lines, shows two of your woodland photographs, and offers a free gift first, "Woodland in winter: a free settings card and 5-minute lesson", then your Academy 101 course.

5

They give their email address and tick the consent box. The card and lesson arrive at once, and your Academy 101 course follows.

6

Over the next ten days they get three short emails in your voice (written by us, approved by you once):

  • Day 0: your card and lesson are here, with your Academy 101 course, and one woodland tip to try this week.
  • Day 4: a short lesson on negative space, with one of your photographs.
  • Day 9: your next winter woodland dates, places left, and the booking link.
7

They book a place. The booking is credited to "How much negative space?" in your weekly report.

8

After the welcome emails they join your main Mailchimp list, tagged "woodland", so your usual campaigns reach them from then on.

Once it is set up, it runs by itself.

Each month we build one more like it for another offer you pick.

Note: YouTube doesn't allow clickable links in Shorts, so on YouTube the short web address on the end card does the job.

03Tracking

How the tracking works

  • Every Short gets its own label: a short web address on YouTube, a keyword on Instagram and Facebook.
  • When someone signs up through it, we record which Short brought them.
  • When a booking comes in, we match the booker's email to that sign-up and credit the Short.

11Good to know

Questions

Will my current subscribers get extra emails?

Only the ones we agree with you: the past-student emails, and in Plan 2 the weekly email. The welcome series only goes to new people.

Do I need to post more Shorts?

No. Your Shorts packs carry on as they are. This works on your pages, your list and the Shorts you already post.

What if my list is small?

We'll know in week one. If the monthly wouldn't pay for itself, we'll tell you straight, and you keep everything from the setup.

Do I need to learn any new software?

No. We run it. You get the report.

What if a booking can't be traced?

It shows as "unmatched" in the report. We don't guess.

Can I stop?

Yes, any month, by email. Everything built stays yours.
