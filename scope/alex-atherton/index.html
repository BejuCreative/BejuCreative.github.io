02How it works

From your footage to 30 Shorts

Two sources, one style, every Short making one clear point.

1

Your existing footage

About 18 to 20 Shorts from your two full talks, your TEDx talk, your podcasts and your recent media. We pick your sharpest opinions, not long stories.

2

Your own takes

About 10 to 12 Shorts from short to-camera recordings, led by your predictions for 2027. You choose the topics, and we send hook ideas to help you plan them.

3

One clear opinion each

Every Short opens fast, makes one point and ends on a complete thought. When it answers a story in the news, that story is on screen from the first second.

4

Ready for every platform

LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, Reels and TikTok, with titles and post text for each Short. No copyrighted music.

Why 30

30 Shorts gives enough to compare hooks and styles. Send us the numbers as they come in, and we tell you which styles to make more of.
