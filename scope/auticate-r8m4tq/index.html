Questions

How do we send our videos?

Send YouTube links, or share your original files in a Drive or Dropbox folder. Original files give the sharpest Shorts.

What if we don't like a Short?

Leave a note in Frame.io, as you do now, and we change it until it's right. Revisions are unlimited.

Do you promise views?

No one honestly can. We promise the quality of the work, and we test and track every Short so the next ones get better.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Your footage stays yours, and every finished Short is yours once the month is paid.

Can we cancel?

Yes. It's month-to-month. Email us before your next billing date. You keep everything delivered. No refunds on delivered work.
