2What you get

Every month on this plan

  • 60 new Shorts a month, made from the strongest parts of your long videos, with hooks and captions built for YouTube Shorts.
  • A folder for each long video, so every Short links straight back to the video it came from.
  • View tracking. We follow how each Short performs on your channel, and what works shapes the next picks, hooks and edits.
  • A views note every month. Around day 28, Simas sends a short written note: how the month's Shorts did, what worked, and what we make more of next.
  • One long video edited on us in your first month. Pick any video, and judge how we handle the full version too.
  • You own every finished video once the month is paid, to post and use as you like.
3How we work together

From your long video to a finished Short

  1. A short kickoff call (15 minutes, optional)

    We confirm your style, the channels, and who approves the Shorts on your side.

  2. You share your long videos

    Drop the original files into a shared folder. You keep filming as you do now.

  3. We pick, edit and polish

    Your first Shorts arrive within 8 hours of getting your videos. Then they keep coming every day.

  4. You leave notes in Frame.io

    The same review project you already use with us. Leave a note on any Short, and we change it until it's right.

  5. Approved Shorts go straight into your folder

    Ready to post, sorted by long video.

  6. Every month, a views note

    What worked, what didn't, and what we make more of next month.
