What you get

  • 60 new Shorts a month, made from the strongest parts of your long videos, with hooks and captions built for YouTube Shorts.
  • A folder for each long video, so every Short links straight back to the video it came from.
  • View tracking. We follow how each Short performs on your channel, and what works shapes the next picks, hooks and edits.
  • One long video edited on us in your first month. Pick any video, and judge how we handle the full version too.
  • You own every finished video once the month is paid, to post and use as you like.

How we work together

  1. A short kickoff call (15 minutes, optional). We confirm your style, the channels and who approves the Shorts on your side.
  2. You share your long videos. Drop the original files into a shared folder. You keep filming as you do now.
  3. We pick, edit and polish. Your first Shorts arrive within 8 hours of getting your videos, then they keep coming every day.
  4. Notes stay in Frame.io, in the same review project you already use with us. Leave a note on any Short, and we change it until it's right.
  5. Approved Shorts go straight into your folder, ready to post, sorted by long video.
  6. A views note every month. Around day 28, Simas sends a short written note: how the month's Shorts did, what worked, and what we make more of next.

What we need from you

  • Your long videos as original files, in a shared Drive or Dropbox folder. Original files give the sharpest Shorts.
  • Your notes in Frame.io, as you give them now.
  • A mobile number for the kickoff call and anything urgent.
