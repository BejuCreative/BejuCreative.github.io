02How it works

From a video to a booked call, step by step

Once it's set up, it runs by itself, every day. You approve the emails, and we keep improving it.

1

Someone watches your video

A Short or a long video. At the end you say one line: "DM me the word GYM". We pick the word and the approach with you.

2

They send the word

In seconds they get a reply on Instagram with a link to your free gift. You don't type anything.

3

They give their email for your free gift

A free guide or a free value video, the one you choose. The first email brings it in seconds.

4

Five emails, written by us, approved by you

Welcome and the gift, a warm email, your client results (how a client changed and how long it took), then an invite to book a call with you. You see every email before it goes live, and you approve what each one says.

5

They book a call

You talk to someone who already knows you and has read your results. Every week you see how many got this far.

Not everybody buys straight from Instagram.

Today, someone who likes your video and moves on is gone. With the engine, they get your gift and hear from you again, until they are ready to book.

Why it gets better every month

Different people get different versions of the emails, for example a different client story. After about 10 people per version, we keep what works and change the rest. Then we keep testing.

09Good to know

Questions

Do I pay for any software?

No. Every tool the engine runs on is paid by our team.

Email didn't work for me before. Why now?

That was a newsletter to everyone. This is different: 5 emails only for people who asked for your gift, leading to one thing, a call with you. And we test and change them until they work.

Who writes the emails?

We do, every one. You approve each one before it goes out. Later, once you trust it, you can tell us to run without asking.

Do I still reach out to people myself?

That's your call. The engine runs next to what you already do. It answers the people who send your word, so you don't have to.

We can also add our DM machine as an extra. We've run it before. It goes through your Instagram followers and the people who engage with your posts, and it writes a personal DM for each one. You read it and send it with one click.

What do I need to do?

Choose your gift, agree the word, approve the emails, send 1 to 3 client results, connect your Instagram, and say your keyword line in your videos.

How will I know it's working?

Every week you see how many people sent your word, signed up and booked a call.

What if I don't like a video or an email?

Tell us on WhatsApp or email, and we fix it right away.

Who owns my list and my videos?

You do. Your list, your emails, your gift, your Instagram and every video stay yours.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Month-to-month. Cancel any time before your next billing date by email. You keep everything delivered. No refunds on delivered work.
