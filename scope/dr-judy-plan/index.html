03Your email campaign

Your email campaign, the way you pictured it

A short free video about your program, then the full introduction in exchange for an email address, then emails that sell the program. Fully automated, so nobody has to manage it.

1

Someone sees your Short

It opens on a question in yellow, and they stop scrolling to hear your answer. Videos about narcissistic abuse lead to Healing Narcissistic Wounds. Videos about childhood wounds lead to Healing Childhood Wounds.

2

They get your short free video

They comment one word. Within seconds, your page sends them a private message with your short free video about the program, and asks them to follow you.

An example from another creator's page. The message is a card with the free gift and one button. A friendly follow-up comes after it, here an invitation to a free class. Yours gets your own words, your free video and your look. We tailor every line with you.

3

They give their email for the full introduction

To see the full introduction, they type their email address, in the message or on your website. It arrives in their inbox at once, and their address joins a list that belongs to you.

4

Your emails sell the program

Three short welcome emails in your voice: the full introduction, one idea from your work, then the invitation to the program. After that, two emails a month keep them close.

5

They buy, or book a free consultation

They buy Healing Narcissistic Wounds or Healing Childhood Wounds, or book a consultation in a calendar, at a time you opened. No call-backs.

Why your video works as a lead magnet

People see and hear you before they buy, so trust starts early. And everyone who asks for the full introduction has already shown interest in that program. Your book can still become a second gift later.

SEO (search engine optimisation): your sites show up when people search Google for help with narcissistic abuse or childhood wounds.

GEO (generative engine optimisation): your sites show up when people ask AI assistants, like ChatGPT or Google's AI answers, the same questions. More and more people search this way.

We set up both on your two program sites, and keep tuning them every month.

10Good to know

Questions

Do I need an IT person? What about HIPAA?

No IT person is needed. Our team sets up and runs every tool. We never collect, see or store patient health information. We only work on your marketing: videos, posts, and the sign-ups of people who ask for your free video. They give a first name and an email address. No health data, no biometric data, nothing private. We never see patient records, and your practice systems stay separate from our tools. Happy to walk you, or anyone you work with, through the tools.

Who owns my accounts, my websites and my email list?

You do. Your social accounts, your websites, your email list and your videos stay yours. We run them for you.

I'm rebuilding my site myself. Does that work?

Yes. Send us what you've built so far. We use it as the starting point, rebuild both program sites properly in your look, and every change waits for your OK.

Why do viewers have to follow my page to get the video?

So that every person who asks for your free video also becomes a follower. Your audience grows with each comment.

What happens with my assistant?

We start alongside her. She shows us how your accounts are set up, and she keeps any part of her work you want her to keep.

Can I move to the higher tier plan later?

Yes. Email us, and it starts from your next billing date. Everything we built keeps running, and nothing is set up twice. The 30-day promise starts the day you pay for the higher tier plan: if no program sells in those 30 days, you choose one more month free or that month back.

Do I pay for any software?

No. Our team pays for and runs every tool we use for you: the message tool, the email sender and the booking calendar.

What if I don't like a video?

Tell us, and we change it until it's right. Revisions are unlimited.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month-to-month. Email us before your next billing date. You keep everything delivered. No refunds on delivered work, except the 30-day promise in the higher tier plan.
