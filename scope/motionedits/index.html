How it works

Four steps, three of them on autopilot

1 · Find

It finds VFX buyers

Every day: indie film and short-film teams in post or crowdfunding VFX, music-video directors, small production companies, and small VFX and post studios hiring roto and comp artists. Each one checked: a real person, an active project, a decision-maker, a verified email.

2 · Write

It writes to them

A short, personal email about their own project, with your proof: 7,500+ shots, 400+ projects, 12+ countries. Sent from separate inboxes, never your own.

3 · Reply

It replies and books the call

It talks to every lead like a real person on your team. It answers questions, handles objections, and steers each conversation toward one goal: a booked call with your salesperson. Your choice: approve replies first, or let them go out on their own.

4 · Close

You close

Your salesperson only talks to people who booked a call. You do the VFX work, they pay you.

Good to know

Questions

How do I know it will work for my business?

You see it before a single email goes out: your lead lists and every email, in the first 2 weeks. Nothing is sent without your green light. After launch you watch it live in your dashboard, and we tune it with you until day 30. No one can honestly promise a number of clients, so we don't.

Will the emails land in spam or hurt my domain?

It's built so that doesn't happen: separate domains, proper authentication, about 2 weeks of warm-up, a slow ramp, verified lists and short personal emails. Your main email and domain are never touched.

How many emails go out?

Fewer, sharper ones. VFX buyers are a smaller, higher-value group, so every email is written for one person. Each inbox stays at 30 a day or less, and you control the volume.

Do my team need to be technical?

No. We build it, run it with you for 30 days, and train your salesperson. Day to day, they approve replies and take the calls.

What happens after day 30?

It keeps running, and you own it. We keep helping until your team runs it really well.

What's not included?

Your VFX work and your sales calls. Paid ads, social media and LinkedIn outreach. And the running costs from month two, paid to the providers.
