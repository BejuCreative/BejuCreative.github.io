What goes into every edit.

Six things we do on each video. All of them follow your own style, not a template.

Hook

We open each video on the line that states the idea.

Cuts

Pauses, repeats and detours go, so the point arrives sooner and still sounds like you.

Captions

Easy to read on a phone with the sound off, set in the look of your channel, with names and terms spelled right.

Sound

Music and sound effects sit under your voice, never over it.

Motion graphics

Titles, highlights and graphics where they help explain the point, in the colours and fonts you already use.

Vertical framing

Wide footage is reframed so the speaker stays in view and captions stay clear of the app buttons.

What is a short-form video editing service?

Beju Creative's short-form video editing service turns long videos you already publish into YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikToks and LinkedIn clips. We choose the strongest moments, then edit the hook, cuts, captions, sound and motion graphics to match your own style. You send recordings, and finished vertical videos come back.

Beju Creative quotes its short-form video editing service per channel. The price depends on how many videos you need each month, how long they are and how much motion design goes into them. Questions.

Which platforms do you edit for?

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn. Tell us where you publish, and we frame and caption each video for it.

Will the Shorts look like my channel?

That is the aim. Send a few of your videos and any references you like, and we edit to match your fonts, colours, pacing and sound. The free sample shows you how close we get before you decide anything.

Do I need to record anything new?

No. We cut from videos you already have, such as a long YouTube video, an interview, a tutorial or a talking-head recording. You send the recordings, and we find the moments worth sharing.

What if I want changes?

Ask for them. Revisions are unlimited on every edit, and we keep editing until you're happy with the result.

Is my footage safe with you?

Yes. Your footage stays private, upload folders are shared only with you, and raw files are deleted once your edits are approved. Payments run through Stripe.
