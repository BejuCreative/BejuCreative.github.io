One clear idea per clip

A short-form edit needs more than subtitles over a talking head. We look for an opening that makes sense without the full recording, cut repetition, and shape the pacing around the point you want to make. Captions, sound design and visual emphasis support that message rather than compete with it.

BEJÚ Creative works with podcasters, coaches and founders using footage they already record. That can be an interview, an educational video or a direct-to-camera explanation.

Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts

Tell us where you plan to publish and whether you need different versions. A vertical clip needs a readable crop, captions away from interface controls, and enough context to stand on its own. We can discuss the right length and format for the material before editing.

  • Hook and pacing: get to the subject quickly without changing what the speaker means.
  • Captions: make the dialogue readable, with names and technical terms checked against your brief.
  • Visual treatment: use framing and supporting graphics where they help explain the idea.
  • Export: confirm destination platforms and required versions before delivery.

What to send for an edit

Share a downloadable footage link, your target audience, the platform you publish on, and one or two references for the style you like. Add brand assets and any words, claims or moments that must stay unchanged. If you have selected timestamps, include those too.

For feedback, a timestamp and a specific change are more useful than “make it punchier.” For example: “At 00:08, remove the repeated sentence and keep the product name on screen.”

Short-form editing questions

Do I need to record something new?

No. You can start with existing footage. Send the source first so we can check whether it has a suitable standalone moment and usable audio.

Can one recording become several clips?

Yes, when it contains several distinct ideas. The useful number depends on the material, not just the recording length. We agree the clip count as part of the brief.
