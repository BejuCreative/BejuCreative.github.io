1. The Service

Beju Creative ("Beju," "we," "us") provides video editing services for clients ("you," "the client"): short-form editing, which turns long-form footage (podcasts, talking-head content, raw clips) into edited short-form videos (Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts), and long-form editing of full-length videos. We quote each order by email, per channel. The price agreed in the accepted quote (section 2.1) is the price that applies to that order.

These terms apply to every order, whichever way you reach us: through our free sample form, by email or by message. By placing an order, you agree to them.

2. Placing an Order

2.1 The free sample, the quote and your order

You start by asking for a free sample through the form at bejucreative.digital/brief. The sample is one Short, with a preview watermark. You may post the sample as it is. The sample carries no payment and no obligation to order, and the clean version comes with a paid order.

After the sample, we send you a quote by email for your channel. A quote names one package: Starter (5 Shorts, one-off), Growth (15 Shorts a month) or Daily (30 Shorts a month), and states the price and what it includes. Your order is placed when you accept the quote in writing (by email) and pay through the payment link we send you. Your answers in the form and your emails with us are your editing brief. We email you to confirm your order within 12 hours, 7 days a week, and always before editing starts. If a link does not work or something is missing, we tell you before the turnaround clock starts. If we cannot do your order as described, we tell you before we start and you can choose a full refund.

2.2 Orders agreed directly with us

If you contact us by email or message instead of through the form, the same steps apply: we confirm the package and the editing brief in writing before you pay.

2.3 What you send us

We need usable footage: a link to your channel or to the exact videos you want edited, or your original files. If your files are not online, tell us in the form or in your reply to the quote and we email you a Google Drive folder to upload them to. We share that upload folder only with your email address, never by link. No Google account? Send a WeTransfer or Dropbox link instead. Work begins after payment and usable footage are received, unless otherwise agreed in writing. If footage is unusable (corrupted, wrong format, missing audio and similar), we ask you to resend it before the turnaround clock starts.

2.4 When a pack expires

The Starter pack (5 Shorts, one-off) expires 14 days after you pay. If by then we still have no usable footage from you, or you have stopped replying to our messages, we close the order: videos we have not made by then lapse and are not refunded. Videos we have already delivered stay yours, and you can still ask for changes to them (section 4). This does not affect your right to cancel before the pack expires: if you cancel before then, section 8 applies, including a full refund if we have not started work and, if you are a consumer, your 14-day right to cancel. Growth, Daily and custom orders follow the timing stated in the quote. Growth and Daily are monthly plans: they are billed in advance and renew every month. You can stop a plan at any time before the next charge. Videos in a month that are not used do not roll over to the next month.

3. Turnaround and Delivery

We deliver within 24 hours, and usually faster, after payment, usable footage and the editing brief are confirmed. That time includes weekends and public holidays: we work 7 days a week. Your form answers and our emails are the brief. Delivery time can extend for unusually large orders or footage that needs significant extra cleanup. We agree any extension upfront.

What "delivered" means

We deliver your finished videos into your Google Drive folder for the order and tell you, by email or message, that they are ready. A video is delivered when it is in that folder and we have told you. Your order is fully delivered when every video in it has been delivered. Google Drive records when each file was added. Your delivery folder is shared by link, so anyone with the link can open it. Please keep the link to yourself.

4. Changes (Revisions)

Revisions are unlimited. Ask for changes to any edit and we keep working on it until you are happy with the result. Revisions cover changes to the edit of the footage you sent; new footage or a new brief counts as a new order.

8. Cancelling an Order

8.1 Before we start

You can cancel any order for a full refund until we start work on it. For the Starter pack, this applies until the pack expires (section 2.4). To cancel, email hello@bejucreative.digital or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram.

8.2 Your 14-day right to cancel (consumers)

If you are buying for yourself and not for a business, you have the right to cancel this contract within 14 days without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day your order is placed (section 2.1). To cancel, tell us clearly by email, WhatsApp or Instagram message, or use the form at the end of this section. You do not have to use the form.

8.3 Starting straight away

We start work as soon as we have your payment and usable footage (section 2), so we can deliver within 24 hours. Our quote email asks you to confirm, in your written acceptance, that you want us to start straight away. If you confirm this, you ask us to start before the 14 days are over. If you do not confirm it, we do not start work until the 14 days have passed. If you cancel after we have started, you pay for the work already done up to the moment you told us: the price of the videos we have already delivered, worked out per video. For example, if you cancel a Starter pack after we have delivered 2 Shorts, you pay 2/5 of the price. We refund the rest within 14 days of hearing from you, to the payment method you used.

8.4 When the right ends

Once we have fully delivered every video in your order (section 3), you can no longer cancel. You confirm this when you accept the quote in writing, and we confirm it again in our order confirmation email.

8.5 If you are buying for a business

The 14-day right in 8.2 does not apply to you. You can cancel for a full refund before we start work. After editing has started, refunds are at our discretion and may account for work already done.
