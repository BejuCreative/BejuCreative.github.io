Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

1. The Service

BEJÚ Creative ("BEJÚ," "we," "us") provides short-form video editing services — turning long-form footage (podcasts, talking-head content, raw clips) into edited short-form videos (Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts) for clients ("you," "the client"). Pricing is volume-based, per our current rate card, and may change over time — the price agreed at the time you place an order is the price that applies to that order.

2. Placing an Order

An order is placed once you confirm a package (number of videos) and send us the raw footage to work from. Work begins once footage is received in a usable format. If footage is unusable (corrupted, wrong format, missing audio, etc.), we'll ask you to resend it before the turnaround clock starts.

3. Turnaround

Standard delivery is 24 hours from when we receive usable footage, for the packages listed on our current rate card. Delivery time can extend for unusually large orders or footage that needs significant extra cleanup — we'll tell you upfront if that's the case rather than surprise you at the deadline.

4. Revisions

Each order includes one round of revisions. Revision requests should be specific and sent within a reasonable time of delivery. Requests that go beyond the original brief (e.g. asking for a substantially different edit rather than a fix) may be treated as a new order.

5. The Guarantee

We offer a performance guarantee: if the edited videos we deliver don't at least double (2×) your average views within 30 days of you posting them, you're entitled to a refund.

  • Refunds are only issued if this specific guarantee is not met. They are not available for general dissatisfaction, change of mind, or reasons unrelated to view performance (for example: you decide not to post the videos, you post them somewhere other than agreed, or you simply no longer want the service after receiving satisfactory work).
  • The 30-day window starts from the date you post the video, not the date we deliver it. Videos that are never posted aren't eligible for a guarantee claim, since performance can't be measured.
  • "Average views" is calculated from your account's own posting history over a comparable recent period, using whatever view data the platform itself reports.
  • To claim, contact us within 35 days of posting with the relevant view data. We'll review in good faith and respond within a reasonable time.

6. Payment

Payment is processed securely through Stripe. We don't store or have access to your full card details. Payment is due before work begins unless otherwise agreed in writing.

You confirm that you have the rights to any footage you send us, and that it doesn't infringe on anyone else's rights. You're responsible for the content of your own videos (claims, statements, etc.) — we're responsible for the editing.

You can cancel an order before we've started work on it for a full refund. Once editing has begun, cancellation refunds are at our discretion and may account for work already completed.
