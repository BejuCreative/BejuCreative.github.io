1. The Service

BEJÚ Creative ("BEJÚ," "we," "us") provides short-form video editing services — turning long-form footage (podcasts, talking-head content, raw clips) into edited short-form videos (Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts) for clients ("you," "the client"). Pricing is volume-based, per our current rate card, and may change over time — the price agreed at the time you place an order is the price that applies to that order.

2. Placing an Order

Before payment, we confirm the package, editing brief and, where applicable, performance-guarantee eligibility and baseline in writing. Work begins after payment and usable footage are received, unless otherwise agreed in writing. If footage is unusable (corrupted, wrong format, missing audio, etc.), we'll ask you to resend it before the turnaround clock starts.

3. Turnaround

Standard short-form package delivery is 24 hours after payment, usable footage and the editing brief are confirmed. For a package covered by the performance guarantee, eligibility and the baseline must be agreed before payment. Delivery time can extend for unusually large orders or footage that needs significant extra cleanup — we agree any extension upfront.

4. Revisions

Each order includes one round of revisions. Revision requests should be specific and sent within a reasonable time of delivery. Requests that go beyond the original brief (e.g. asking for a substantially different edit rather than a fix) may be treated as a new order.

5. The Performance Guarantee

Double your agreed baseline average views, or receive a full refund of the qualifying short-form package payment. This is a conditional performance guarantee, not an unconditional satisfaction guarantee.

5.1 Covered orders and eligibility

All paid short-form packages can qualify, including one-, three- and five-clip orders. Long-form editing and launch films are excluded. Before payment, we confirm eligibility and record the account, platform, comparison clips, baseline, package clip count and target in writing. You need at least five comparable previously published clips and sufficient platform analytics to establish their first-30-day organic views. The free sample is a separate one-time offer with no payment or purchase obligation; it is not included in the paid package’s result.

5.2 Baseline and result

The baseline is the arithmetic average of the first-30-day organic views of your previous 10 comparable clips on the agreed account and platform. If fewer than 10 are available, we use five. We agree the comparison clips and their data before payment. The target is twice that baseline.

For the delivered package, add each clip’s organic views during its own first 30 days after posting, then divide by the number of clips in the package. If that average is below the target and the remaining conditions are met, the full amount paid for that package is refundable. Individual clips do not each have to double; a one-clip order is measured on that clip alone. For example, a 1,000-view baseline gives a 2,000-view package-average target. A qualifying $75 order that misses the target receives a $75 refund.

5.3 Posting and measurement

Publish every delivered clip within 14 calendar days of delivery on the agreed account and platform. For larger packages, a written posting schedule agreed before payment replaces the 14-day deadline; all clips must be posted by their agreed dates. Keep every clip public and unchanged, without paid promotion, throughout its own first 30 days. The measurement clock starts at posting, not delivery. The package result is final 30 days after the last clip is posted. Unposted, deleted, private, altered or promoted clips do not satisfy these conditions; publishing on another account or platform does not count towards the agreed test.

5.4 Claiming a refund

Email bejusipe@gmail.com within seven calendar days after the final posted clip completes its 30-day window. Include your order details, links and posting dates for every delivered clip, and platform analytics showing each clip’s first-30-day organic views. We compare those results with the recorded baseline, review the claim in good faith and confirm the outcome by email. If the conditions are met and the target was missed, we refund the full qualifying package payment, not credits or vouchers.

5.5 Revisions and repeat orders

One revision round is included under section 4. General dissatisfaction, an editing-style preference or a change of mind alone does not trigger this performance refund. Your agreed baseline remains fixed for three months from the baseline agreement date; we reassess it before further orders after that period. Each package is evaluated separately against the baseline recorded for that order. We do not promise to double the previous month repeatedly, and a new baseline does not change an existing order’s target.

This voluntary performance guarantee does not limit any rights that apply under law. Existing orders remain subject to the terms agreed when they were placed.
