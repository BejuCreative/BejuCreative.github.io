What does video editing for coaches include?

Video editing for coaches at Beju Creative means turning your calls, talks, webinars and long videos into Shorts and Reels for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn. Questions from coaches.

Can you edit recordings of calls with my clients?

Only if your client has agreed to it. Send us recordings you have permission to use, and tell us what has to stay private, such as names, details or screens. We cut around it.

Will you keep my wording and my technical terms?

Yes. Tell us which terms, qualifications or limits must stay in, which matters most in fitness, health and business advice. We edit for clarity and we do not rewrite what you said.

Can you edit a demonstration, like an exercise or a technique?

Yes. Show us what has to stay in frame, such as the movement or the object. We build the vertical crop around it and add labels where they help the explanation.

Do you edit long videos as well as Shorts?

Yes. We edit full-length videos for your channel as well as the Shorts cut from them. If your long video is a conversation with a guest, see our podcast clip editing page.
