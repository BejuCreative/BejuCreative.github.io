Questions from course creators.

Can you cut Shorts from my lessons and webinars?

Yes. Send lessons, live webinars, workshop recordings or Q&A calls. We find the parts that stand on their own, such as one idea, one answer or one demonstration, and cut each into a Short that makes sense without the rest of the lesson.

Will the Shorts give away my course?

We choose moments that show how you teach, not the whole method. Tell us what belongs to the paid course and what you are happy to share, and we keep to that.

Do you edit the lessons themselves?

Yes. We edit full-length videos, including lesson and webinar recordings. Tell us in your application what each one needs.

Can you make a film for my course launch?

Yes. We make launch films for SaaS and app launches, and we make them for courses too. Tell us when the course opens and what you want people to do, and we plan the film around that.

How does the free sample work?

We edit a Short from a video you already have and send it to you. No payment, no card. We review every application by hand, so we cannot promise a sample to everyone.
