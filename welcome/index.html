Better footage, better clips

If your footage is already online, the links you gave us are enough. If it isn't, upload your files to the Google Drive upload folder we email you. It's shared only with your email address. No Google account, no folder email yet, or prefer another way? Send a WeTransfer, Dropbox or Google Drive link by email or WhatsApp. Step-by-step upload help is in the client guide. Packs expire 14 days after you pay, so send your footage before then (section 2.4 of our terms).

  • Send the original file. Export straight from your camera, Riverside, Zoom or editing app. Files sent through WhatsApp, Instagram or a screen recording lose most of their quality.
  • Longer is fine. Send the whole recording: one good episode can become a month of clips.
  • Separate cameras or audio? Send every angle and audio track you have. More angles means more cutting options.
  • Got favourite moments? Send us the timestamps and we'll start there.
  • Keep your originals. We delete your raw footage once you've approved your edits.
  • Big files take a while. Keep the tab open until the upload says it's complete. The delivery clock (24 hours, and usually faster) starts once we have usable footage. If a file is corrupted or missing audio, we'll ask you to resend it.

Feedback and revisions

Revisions are unlimited. We keep editing until you're happy. Send your changes together within 48 hours of delivery. Changes usually come back the same day. To get exactly what you want in one go:

  • Send all your changes together. Leave timestamped comments on the videos in Google Drive (in a desktop browser), or list every note for every clip in one message. How to comment in Drive.
  • Use timestamps. "0:12" is faster and clearer than "near the start".
  • Say what you liked, too. It tells us what to keep doing in your next pack.

Example of great feedback
Clip 2, 0:04: cut the pause
Clip 2, 0:18: her name is spelled "Sara"
Clip 4: use the other hook
Loved clip 3's captions: more like that

Fixes and tweaks are covered by your revisions. A completely different edit from the original brief may be treated as a new order. See section 4 of our terms.

How to post your clips

  • Post consistently. Several clips a week beats a burst of five on one day and then silence.
  • Upload natively to each platform. Upload the file to each app directly rather than sharing a link or cross-posting.
  • Write a caption with a hook. A short line that makes people want to watch, plus the words people would search for.
  • Reply to comments early. Engagement in the first hour helps the clip reach more people.
  • Don't delete slow starters. Some clips pick up days later.
  • Screenshot your views. Handy for tracking what works.
