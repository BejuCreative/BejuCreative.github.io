Sending footage

Better footage,
better clips

Open the folder link from our email, then drag your files in. On your phone: open it in the Google Drive app, tap +, then Upload.

  • Send the original file. Export straight from your camera, Riverside, Zoom or editing app. Files sent through WhatsApp, Instagram or a screen recording lose most of their quality.
  • Longer is fine. Send the whole recording — one good episode can become a month of clips.
  • Separate cameras or audio? Upload every angle and audio track you have. More angles means more cutting options.
  • Got favourite moments? Send us the timestamps and we'll start there.
  • Big files take a while. Keep the tab open until Drive says the upload is complete. The 24-hour clock starts once we have usable footage — if a file is corrupted or missing audio, we'll ask you to resend it.

Feedback & revisions

One round.
Make it count.

Every order includes one round of revisions, and changes come back the same day. To get exactly what you want in one go:

  • Send all your changes in one message. List every note for every clip together.
  • Use timestamps. "0:12" is faster and clearer than "near the start".
  • Say what you liked, too. It tells us what to keep doing in your next pack.
Example of great feedback

Clip 2 — 0:04 cut the pause
Clip 2 — 0:18 her name is spelled "Sara"
Clip 4 — use the other hook
Loved clip 3's captions — more like that

Fixes and tweaks are covered by your revision round. A completely different edit from the original brief may be treated as a new order — see our terms.

Get the most views

How to post
your clips

  • Post consistently. Several clips a week beats a burst of five on one day and then silence.
  • Upload natively to each platform. Upload the file to each app directly rather than sharing a link or cross-posting.
  • Write a caption with a hook. A short line that makes people want to watch, plus the words people would search for.
  • Reply to comments early. Engagement in the first hour helps the clip reach more people.
  • Don't delete slow starters. Some clips pick up days later.
  • Screenshot your views. Handy for tracking what works — and you'll need them if you ever claim the guarantee.
